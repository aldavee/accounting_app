# Accounting App (Android & Desktop)

Offline-first double-entry accounting fundamentals app built with Flutter for **Android**, **Windows**, and **Linux**.

## Fundamentals Included

- **Chart of Accounts**: Assets, Liabilities, Equity, Revenue, and Expenses with normal balance tracking (`Debit` vs `Credit`).
- **Double-Entry Journal Entries**: Multi-line Debit/Credit entries with automatic `Debits == Credits` validation plus quick entry templates (Cash Sale, Pay Expense, Owner Capital, Credit Invoice).
- **General Ledger**: Per-account running balance postings.
- **Financial Reports**: Trial Balance, Income Statement (Profit & Loss), Balance Sheet, and live Accounting Equation verification (`Assets = Liabilities + Equity`).
- **Offline-First + Google Drive Backup & OTA Updates**: Local atomic JSON storage, clipboard backup export/import, Google Drive sync, Biometric App Lock, and Google Drive/Portal self-updater.
